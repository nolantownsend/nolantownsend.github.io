I'm a senior water resources analyst at the City of Westminster, Colorado, where I work on water accounting, water rights, analytics, and the dashboards and automation that tie them together.

I've spent more than ten years in water resources, starting in the field as a hydrogeologist and moving into hydrological modeling, climate scenario assessment and digital tools. Most of that work has been for utilities and resource managers across the West, helping them make data-informed decisions about supply reliability, drought planning and system operations.

## Where I've worked

- **City of Westminster** · Senior Water Resources Analyst · water accounting, water rights, dashboards and automation, analytics
- **INTERA** · Water Resources Scientist · water efficiency planning, groundwater pumping impacts, SWAT+ workflows and Dash dashboards for basin-scale recharge
- **Hazen and Sawyer** · Digital Solutions Developer and Water Resources Engineer · unimpaired inflow methods, OASIS modeling, Power BI and Power Platform tools, CMIP5/CMIP6 climate vulnerability analysis
- **University of New Mexico** · Research Assistant · climate impacts on the Rio Grande and downstream water users
- **Pastor Behling and Wheeler** · Staff II Hydrogeologist · site characterization and remediation

## Education

- M.S., Earth & Planetary Sciences, University of New Mexico
- B.S., Geological Science, University of Texas at Austin

## Tools I use

Modeling with WEAP, OASIS, RiverWare, HEC-HMS and SWAT+. GIS in ArcGIS Pro and Python (geopandas, rasterio). Dashboards in Power BI, MS Fabric and Plotly Dash. Development in Python, SQL, Git and the Power Platform.
